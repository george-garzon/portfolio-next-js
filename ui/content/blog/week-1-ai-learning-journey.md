# Week 1 AI Learning Journey: Building the Foundation

Week 1 was about getting past the surface-level excitement around AI and turning it into practical momentum. I wanted to understand how to use AI as a real building partner, not just as a tool for quick answers.

The biggest accomplishment was learning how to break work down into smaller decisions: what I wanted to build, what the app needed to communicate, and which pieces mattered first. That helped me move from vague ideas into concrete screens, routes, copy, and structure.

I also started getting more comfortable with the rhythm of working with AI:

- Describe the goal clearly.
- Review what already exists in the project.
- Ask for a focused change.
- Test the result and tighten the details.

By the end of the week, I had a better workflow for using AI inside a real codebase. I was able to make progress on my portfolio, think through product presentation, and start treating each small build as a learning loop.

The main lesson was simple: AI is most useful when I bring direction. The clearer I am about the outcome, the better it can help me move from an idea to something real.
