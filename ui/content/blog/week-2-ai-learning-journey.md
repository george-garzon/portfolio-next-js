# Week 2 AI Learning Journey: Shipping a Real App Surface

Week 2 was about applying the workflow to something more concrete: building out a public surface for InstaDL, my iOS app for saving and managing content that inspires me.

The work became more practical this week. Instead of only exploring ideas, I started shaping an app presence with pages that a real user would expect:

- A focused landing page that explains what InstaDL does.
- A support page with a clear contact path.
- A shared layout with navigation between the app pages.
- A portfolio section that presents InstaDL alongside my other work.

This helped me understand how AI fits into product work. It can help draft structure, refine copy, create routes, and keep implementation moving, but I still need to make the product decisions: what the app is, who it is for, and what belongs on the page.

The biggest improvement from week 1 was confidence. I was no longer just asking AI questions. I was using it to build, inspect, adjust, and ship small pieces of a real project.

Week 2 made the learning feel more grounded. The result was not just notes or theory. It was a working app section in my portfolio and a clearer sense of how to keep using AI as part of my development process.
